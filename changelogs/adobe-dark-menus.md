## 1.1.0 ([Nov 14, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/c8081b42eb681f51cbc8bd85d68ae5da00319ea7/mods/adobe-dark-menus.wh.cpp))

* Fixed a crash on exit, seen in InDesign and Audition.
* Fixed the scroll arrows of long menus when Menu Background Color is mid gray or lighter.
* Fixed submenus drawn by Windows taking the custom background of an app-drawn parent menu.
* Fixed dark title bars on right-to-left windows.
* Fixed a case where dropdown list colors could stay on after the list was gone.
* No more arrow flicker after scrolling a long menu with the keyboard, and less work while the mouse moves over one.
* Safer when the mod is turned off or updated while an app is running.
* Added a Scroll Long Menus Instead of Columns setting, on by default: a menu an app splits into columns, such as the After Effects Effect menu with many plugins installed, now opens as one dark column that scrolls instead of staying light.
* Added InCopy, Lightroom and Lightroom Classic.
* Added the Beta versions of Premiere Pro, InDesign, InCopy and Audition.
