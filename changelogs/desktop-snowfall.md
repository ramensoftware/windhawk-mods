## 1.2 ([Nov 20, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/21ab62591ab5ab1f5a1fea30751db35e97370152/mods/desktop-snowfall.wh.cpp))

* The mod is now called Desktop Weather. It's the same mod, and your settings are kept.
* New "What falls" setting: snow, rain, autumn leaves or cherry blossom petals. Rain splashes on top of the windows. Leaves and petals rest on top of the windows for a while and then fade away. They ride along when you move a window, and fly off when you yank or close it or sweep them with the cursor.
* "Hide in fullscreen apps" now works on every monitor, not only the one with the focus: a fullscreen video on one monitor hides the snow there while you work on another one. It also recognizes more fullscreen players and games.
* New "Also hide over maximized windows" setting (off by default), handy with an auto-hidden taskbar, where a maximized window fills the whole screen.
* Choose the monitors: all, only the primary one, or a list of monitor numbers. Each monitor can have its own settings (what falls, how much, and where).
* Choose the color of the snow and rain (presets or a custom color code), and the opacity of everything that falls.
* New hotkey to turn it off and on, Win+Alt+S by default.
* New "Hide for these apps" setting: hide it on a monitor while a window of one of the listed apps is open there.
* It can come only for a season (for example from December to February) or only at certain hours. While it's off, it frees its video memory.
* A new demo shows all four kinds of weather.

## 1.1 ([Nov 17, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/a44144776438bb944427d5cb39f50f57eafe74ce/mods/desktop-snowfall.wh.cpp))

* New "Where the snow falls" setting: over all windows, or behind them on the desktop. Behind windows, the falling snow shows only where the desktop is uncovered, and the snow still settles on top of the windows and the taskbar.
* Much lower power use. Calm snowfall is drawn at about 30 fps, and the full frame rate is used only while something moves. Snow that lies still isn't redrawn. The frame rate is capped on battery and lowered in energy saver mode. The snow pauses while the screen is locked or off.
* Each monitor gets its own snow, sized for its display scale. A fullscreen app hides the snow only on its own monitor.
* Shake a window to throw its snow off. Closing or minimizing a window drops its snow in lumps that burst into powder.
* Snow piles up along the bottom of the screen where the taskbar doesn't cover it (new setting, on by default).
* New "Snow slowly melts" setting.
* New "Hide from screenshots and screen sharing" setting.
* Menus, tooltips and the shell's flyouts now stay in front of the snow, and popup windows hide the snow lying behind them.
