## 1.2 ([Nov 14, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/06bb6df5b1aebfa91ab73c3e16ec5c616d71d794/mods/compact-notifications.wh.cpp))

* Cards come straight from the notification database, so toasts that arrive in a row show together, and an app updating a shown toast updates its card.
* Replies are typed on the card itself and sent with the app's button (or Enter).
* Copy button for one-time codes (in the Windows language, or English via a setting).
* Mute button (moon, on hover): mutes an app for 15 min, 1 h, 8 h, 24 h or for good (∞); a new setting unmutes the apps muted for good.
* Do not disturb on Windows 11 is respected.
* Cards stay while the mouse is over them; a card goes as soon as one of its buttons is pressed.
* Animations follow the monitor's refresh rate; new optional playful style.
* Chat text formatting (bold, italic, underline, strike, code) can be turned off.
* Sound plays when the card shows instead of when Windows gets to the banner later; new Sound settings (when the card shows, as Windows plays it, or none) and your own .wav file.
* Fixes: a closed card no longer comes back, the card limit keeps reminders, alarms and calls, cards go when the app takes its toast back (e.g. a call answered in the app).
