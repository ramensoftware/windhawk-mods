## 1.2 ([Nov 18, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/624914d4ab53e3effc884f667821807179a8c8f5/mods/alt-drag.wh.cpp))

* Resizing the window from its center now sizes it in all directions at once, keeping its center in place. This new functionality is optional and can be disabled in the mod settings.
* Fixed the cursor not updating or updating incorrectly after the drag in some cases.
* Fixed compatibility with some programs. One notable example is Notepad.
* Other minor fixes and improvements.

The first two items and some of the fixes were contributed by [Lone](https://github.com/Louis047).

## 1.1 ([Sep 15, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/09d24cc8eb03bb94ebb7d7f79158dacc9c9d2902/mods/alt-drag.wh.cpp))

* Added the ability to resize any window by holding Alt and dragging it with the right mouse button.
* The mouse buttons and the keys can now be changed in the mod settings.
* A delay can now be configured, such that move/resize only start when the mouse is held for the specified amount of time. A shorter press behaves as a regular click.
