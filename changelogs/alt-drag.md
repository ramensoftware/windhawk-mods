## 1.1 ([Sep 15, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/09d24cc8eb03bb94ebb7d7f79158dacc9c9d2902/mods/alt-drag.wh.cpp))

* Added the ability to resize any window by holding Alt and dragging it with the right mouse button.
* The mouse buttons and the keys can now be changed in the mod settings.
* A delay can now be configured, such that move/resize only start when the mouse is held for the specified amount of time. A shorter press behaves as a regular click.
