## 1.1.0 ([Nov 14, 2026](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/589c9d0677cb6e995c3f1070e40f2e3aea7822fb/mods/lock-screen-live-wallpaper.wh.cpp))

- Preview window: check fit, colors and speed without locking the PC. Open it from the settings, an optional hotkey (off by default) or an optional tray icon. It can also draw the real clock, date and status icons over the video (optional, off by default).
- Animated GIF support, alone or inside a folder.
- Low battery saver: lower the frame rate or the render width, or freeze on a still frame.
- Dim setting to darken the video.
- Folder order: random, sequential or fixed.
- Audio fade in and out.
- Still frame duration after boot is now a setting.
- Cached first frames older than 30 days are deleted, and only the 5 newest others are kept.
- Faster color processing when only brightness, contrast, gamma or dim change.
- Fixes: padded decoder frame height (for example 1088 for 1080), audio restarting together with the video on every loop, crash guard no longer counts quick unlocks as failed starts.
